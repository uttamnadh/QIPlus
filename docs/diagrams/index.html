⚙ Verified Multi-Role Rules & Decision Paths
1. Onboarding Officer (Sukesh) Data Intake
  • Completes Steps 1 to 8: Profile, Business, Ownership, UBOs, Signatories, Banking, Documents, Review.
  • Submitting Step 8 generates a unique Merchant Reference Number (MRN).
  • Clarification Loop: If returned by Compliance, appears in the dedicated Returned for clarification list (NOT Drafts).
  • Officer clicks Resume editing, modifies requested fields, and re-submits via Step 8.
2. Compliance Officer (bhanu) 4 Decisions
  • 1. Return for Clarification: Moves to Onboarding Officer's clarification queue with remarks (never Drafts).
  • 2. Reject: Immediately moves record to Universal Rejected List (visible in all 3 roles).
  • 3. Compliance On-Hold: Pauses in Compliance On-Hold list; released via Release from Hold to resume verification.
  • 4. Approve & Forward: Dispatches to eMcREY AML Screening Engine and forwards to Final Approval Queue.
3. eMcREY Screening Engine 3 Outcomes
  • CLEAR: AML check passed without hits; decision actions are unlocked for Final Approver.
  • ON-HOLD: Screening held pending external analysis; approver decision buttons/notes are LOCKED.
  • HIT: Sanctions/PEP match found; approver decision buttons/notes are LOCKED.
  • Approver cannot take any decision while screening status is ON-HOLD or HIT.
4. Final Approver (uttamnadh) 3 Decisions
  • 1. Direct Approve & Activate: Available for CLEAR screening; activates merchant in Live Directory.
  • 2. Final On-Hold: Moves back to Compliance Verification Queue where Compliance must Release On-Hold before returning to Approver to activate.
  • 3. Reject: Rejects with rationale; enters Universal Rejected List across all 3 roles.
